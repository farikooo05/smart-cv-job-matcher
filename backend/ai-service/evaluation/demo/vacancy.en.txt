About the vacancy
This intern role involves taking part in the validation of credit risk, IFRS 9, Machine Learning and Gen AI models, as well as preparing data and checking its quality. Candidates are expected to know the basics of Python, SQL, statistics and machine learning, and to understand Gen AI, LLMs, RAG and AI agents.

Requirements
Higher education in mathematics, statistics, computer science, data science or a similar field (final-year student, graduate or master's student)
Knowledge of Python and SQL, and the basics of statistics and machine learning
Understanding of Gen AI and LLM-based solutions (prompt writing, RAG, AI agents); practical experience is an advantage
English: at a level sufficient to read technical literature and prepare documents
Analytical and critical thinking, attention to detail
Ability to learn quickly, a sense of responsibility and the ability to work in a team

Responsibilities
Support validators in the independent validation of models (credit risk, IFRS 9, ML and Gen AI models);
Prepare data for validation, check its quality and carry out initial analysis;
Run validation tests (discriminatory power, calibration, stability, backtesting) using Python and SQL;
Take part in testing AI/Gen AI models and AI agents (answer correctness, hallucination, bias, robustness);
Support the automation of validation processes with AI tools;
Take part in preparing validation reports and working documents;
Comply with confidentiality and information security requirements.

What we offer
Gain practical experience by taking part in real projects and tasks;
Learn from professional specialists and apply your knowledge in practice;
Develop teamwork, communication and problem-solving skills;
Get to know a corporate working environment closely and define your career interests;
Join the Bir ecosystem's talent pool for future career opportunities.
